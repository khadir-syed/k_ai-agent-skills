AI skills, explained

Imagine giving a new team member a recipe card: "When someone asks for X, do these steps, and never do that." They follow it the same way every time. A skill is that recipe card — for an AI helper.

An agent follows a few recipe cards in a row to finish a bigger job. Some agents check with you after every step; others only stop before they save or send something.

Every page here shows one job in plain words, with a real run: what we asked a real AI, and exactly what it answered (only personal details are ever taken out). No coding needed.

Follow one story

Sales asks the product team for "an export button on the reports page". That's all they say. Here's how AI skills help turn that into a feature that's ready to launch:

Job 1 Something's unclear? Find what's missing Before anyone writes a plan, the AI lists the questions nobody has answered yet — and who could answer them. Job 2 Write me a first draft The AI writes a first draft from what you give it, shows it to you, and only saves it once you say yes. Job 3 Is it ready to go? Like a pilot's checklist before take-off: the AI runs the same 6 checks every time. You still decide whether to launch. Job 4 Do the whole job with me An agent does jobs 1, 2 and 3 in a row. See the version that asks you after every step next to the one that asks only once.
