Do the whole job with me

Same job, two agents: one asks after every step, one asks once

Think of two people helping you. A new intern checks with you after every task: "Done with this — shall I carry on?" An experienced colleague does the routine parts alone, but still asks before sending anything out in your name.

An agent is an AI that does several steps in a row. Both agents here do the same 3 skill jobs: find what's missing, write a first draft (a short launch plan, saved as a file) and run the 6 launch checks. The only difference is how often they stop to ask you.

One job, two ways

Controlled stops after every step. It even refuses "just do it all at once" — the stops are the whole point of this version.

Autonomous doesn't stop between routine steps, but it still asks before it saves the file. Fewer stops doesn't mean fewer rules.

The lesson: "autonomous" is not a yes/no switch. It's a choice about where a person checks the work — and saving or sending something is always one of those places.
