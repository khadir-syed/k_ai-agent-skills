Find what's missing

Ask the questions before anyone writes the plan

Imagine you tell a builder: "I want a new kitchen." A good builder doesn't start knocking down walls. First they ask: gas or electric? How many people cook here? Where do the plugs go? And they tell you which answers only you can give.

This skill is that good builder, for a feature request. Someone asks for something in one line. Before anyone writes a plan, the AI lists what's actually known, what's missing, and who could fill each gap.

What it does — and what it won't do

It does:

  • Lists the questions nobody has answered yet
  • Marks every fact: written down, someone said it, a guess, or unknown
  • Shows two readings when the request could mean two things
  • Says who could answer each question

It won't:

  • Write the plan (that's Job 2)
  • Pick an answer for you and pretend it was asked for
  • Settle a disagreement between people for you

A good answer vs a weak one

For the request below, this is what we built the skill to do — and the kind of answer it must avoid.

  • ✅ "Which file type — spreadsheet (CSV), PDF, or both? Which report, if the page has several? The phone app too? Should everyone see the button, or only some people?"

    Good: it names exactly what nobody has said yet.

  • ✅ "This could mean a one-click download of what you see now, or a report emailed to you every week."

    Good: two honest readings, and it doesn't pick one for you.

  • ❌ "Add a CSV export button."

    Weak: nobody asked for CSV. It made that up and dressed it as a fact.

  • ❌ "This is unclear."

    Weak: it doesn't say which parts are unclear, or who could clear them up.

The lesson: a good first step doesn't guess. It turns "I want an export" into a short list of questions for the right people.

What we gave the AI

The request:

Use the Requirements Gap Investigator. Sales asked for "an export button on the reports page." Figure out what's actually needed before we write it up.

The only background it had — a chat message and a ticket:

Slack message from Sales lead (quoted):
  "Can we get an export on the reports page? Customers keep asking."

Linked ticket description (written by a PM):
  "Export button for reports page."

No further detail in the ticket. No mention of file format, which
report(s), permissions, or whether this applies to the mobile app.
